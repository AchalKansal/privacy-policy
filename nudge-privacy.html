This Privacy Policy describes how the Nudge app ("the App"), developed and published by Offline Coders, handles user data. This policy applies specifically to the Nudge app available on Google Play under the developer account "Offline Coders."

Data Collection

Nudge does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information. All data you create in the App — including task titles, notes, due dates, reminder preferences, and digest settings — is stored exclusively in local storage on your device.

Nudge does not:

  • Require account creation or login
  • Connect to the internet (the App requests no internet permission)
  • Use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Show advertisements
  • Collect device identifiers, location, or usage data
  • Share any data with third parties or third-party services

Permissions Used

The App requests certain device permissions solely to provide its core reminder functionality, not to collect data:

  • Notifications and exact alarms — to alert you at the time you set for a task
  • Boot start — to restore your scheduled reminders after your device restarts
  • Full-screen alerts, vibration, and wake/lock screen display — so a full-screen reminder reliably reaches you, even if your device is locked

If you choose a custom reminder tone, the App reads only the sound file you pick through Android's own system tone picker; it never scans or accesses your other files. None of these permissions are used to transmit information off your device.

Data Storage and Control

All data created within the App remains on your device at all times and is under your full control. Deleting a task or uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data. Offline Coders has no access to any data you create in the App.

Children's Privacy

Nudge does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13, as it collects no data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
