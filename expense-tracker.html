This Privacy Policy describes how the Expense Tracker app ("the App"), developed and published by Offline Coders, handles user data. This policy applies specifically to the Expense Tracker app available on Google Play under the developer account "Offline Coders."

Data Collection

Expense Tracker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information. All expense entries, categories, and history you create in the App are stored exclusively in local storage on your device.

Expense Tracker does not:

  • Require account creation or login
  • Connect to the internet
  • Use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Show advertisements
  • Collect device identifiers, location, or usage data
  • Share any data with third parties or third-party services

Data Storage and Control

All data created within the App remains on your device at all times and is under your full control. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data. Offline Coders has no access to any data you create in the App.

Children's Privacy

Expense Tracker does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13, as it collects no data at all.
