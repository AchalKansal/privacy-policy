This Privacy Policy describes how the Medicine Reminder app ("the App"), developed and published by Offline Coders, handles user data. This policy applies specifically to the Medicine Reminder app available on Google Play under the developer account "Offline Coders."

Data Collection

Medicine Reminder does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information. All reminder data you create in the App — including medicine names, reminder times, optional place notes, and optional medicine photos — is stored exclusively in local storage on your device.

Medicine Reminder does not:

  • Require account creation or login
  • Connect to the internet
  • Use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Show advertisements
  • Collect device identifiers, location, or usage data
  • Share any data with third parties or third-party services

Permissions Used

The App requests certain device permissions solely to provide its core reminder functionality, not to collect data:

  • Notifications and exact alarms — to alert you at the medicine time you set
  • Boot start — to restore your scheduled reminders after your device restarts
  • Full-screen alerts, vibration, and wake/lock screen display — so the reminder alarm reliably reaches you, even if your device is locked
  • Photos — only if you choose to attach a photo of a medicine to a reminder; the photo is stored locally and never uploaded anywhere

None of these permissions are used to transmit information off your device.

Health-Related Information

Medicine names and related notes you enter may be considered health-related information. This information never leaves your device — it is not transmitted to Offline Coders, to the App itself over any network, or to any third party, because the App has no network access at all.

Data Storage and Control

All data created within the App remains on your device at all times and is under your full control. Deleting a reminder or uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data. Offline Coders has no access to any data you create in the App.

Children's Privacy

Medicine Reminder does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13, as it collects no data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
