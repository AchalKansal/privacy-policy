Privacy Policy for BabyBuddy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Who operates BabyBuddy

BabyBuddy is provided by Offline Coders. For privacy questions, contact offlinecoders@gmail.com.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the BabyBuddy Android app (package com.babybuddy.caretracker) and the Baby & Me website it displays at achalkansal.github.io/baby-and-me/. The app loads the website over the internet; it is not a fully offline app. It does not require an account.

Information you enter and create

You may enter or create feeding and diaper logs, sleep entries, a baby's birth date, milestone and vaccination checklists, food and teething records, and first-moment photos. The current website code stores these records in local storage on your device. Offline Coders does not receive or sync these records to its own server. The current app and website do not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Please do not put information in BabyBuddy that you are uncomfortable keeping on your device. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or its app data may be able to see it.

Camera and photo access

The Mirror feature requests camera access when you start it. Its live camera view is displayed on your device; the feature does not record or upload video. You can deny or revoke camera access in Android Settings.

Baby Firsts lets you choose or capture a photo using your device's file or camera chooser. The selected image is processed and saved in the app's local storage. If you choose to share or export a photo or collage, the destination app or service you select will handle the copy you send under its own privacy practices. BabyBuddy does not automatically upload your photos.

Website hosting and technical data

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Each time the app loads or refreshes a page, your device makes an HTTPS request to GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, device and browser information, and request or usage logs to deliver and secure its service. Offline Coders does not use those hosting requests to receive your baby-care records. GitHub's handling of technical data is described in its Privacy Statement.

Links that leave the Baby & Me website may open in another app or browser. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

How information is used

Information saved locally is used to display the trackers, checklists, memories, and other features you choose to use. Camera access supports Mirror; photo access supports Baby Firsts. The app does not sell your information or use your baby-care records for advertising.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Your entries and saved photos remain in the app's local storage until you delete them in a feature (where available), clear BabyBuddy's app storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove this information; BabyBuddy does not provide a cloud backup or account-based recovery. The Android app disables system backup of its app data.

Offline Coders cannot remotely view or delete records stored only on your device. GitHub controls retention of any technical information it processes as the website host, as explained in its privacy statement.

Children's privacy

BabyBuddy is intended for parents and caregivers, although some play features may be shown to a child with adult supervision. The app does not provide child accounts. A caregiver may choose to store information about a child, including a birth date, care records, or photos, locally on the device. We do not knowingly ask children to submit contact information or send their care records to us. If you have a concern about a child's information, contact us using the address below.

Security

The website is loaded over HTTPS. Care records and photos are kept in the app's local storage rather than sent to an Offline Coders server. The app does not add its own encryption or password lock to that local storage, so please use your device's screen lock and avoid sharing access to your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may choose not to enter care information, add photos, or grant camera access. You can revoke camera access in Android Settings. You can remove locally stored information as described above. Sharing or exporting content is optional and initiated by you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if BabyBuddy's features, hosting, or data practices change. We will post the revised policy at the same location and update its effective date. Material changes to data handling should be reflected in the app and its Google Play Data safety information before release.
